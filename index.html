FAQ & Answers

Answers to what people usually ask before booking a clean

What areas do you service?

We proudly service Sydney's Lower North Shore, Northern Beaches, and CBD & Inner City. Not sure if we cover your suburb? Get in touch and we'll let you know.

What cleaning services do you offer?

General home cleaning, deep cleaning, end of lease cleaning and commercial/office cleaning — see the Services section above for details on each.

How do I get a quote?

Fill out the quote form below with a few details about your space and what you need cleaned, and we'll get back to you to confirm pricing and availability.
Get your free quote →

Do I need to provide cleaning products or equipment?

No need — we bring all the cleaning products and equipment. If you'd prefer us to use your own instead, just let us know when you request a quote.

Do I need to be home when you attend?

No, you don't need to be home — just let us know how we can access your property (a key, code, or via a neighbour or agent) and we'll take care of the rest. If you'd prefer to be there, that's fine too.
